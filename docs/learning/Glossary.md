# Cyberussell Glossary

Version: 1.0

---

## Academy

The complete Cyberussell learning ecosystem.

---

## Learning Path

A structured sequence of Pillars that guides learners from beginner to practical AI proficiency.

Example:
AI Foundations → Think with AI → Meet Your AI Team → AI Workflows → Build Real Skills

---

## Pillar

A major area of learning focused on a broad competency.

Examples:
- AI Foundations
- Think with AI
- Meet Your AI Team

A Pillar contains multiple Guides.

---

## Guide

The smallest learning unit.

A Guide teaches one concept and includes an interactive exercise.

Examples:
- What AI Really Is
- AI Hallucinations
- Prompt Basics

---

## Exercise

A short hands-on activity completed within a Guide.

Exercises reinforce learning through practice.

---

## Mission

A practical project that combines multiple Guides into a real-world outcome.

Examples:
- Create Your First Resume
- Build a Landing Page
- Research a Business Idea

---

## Challenge

An optional premium assessment or portfolio project that demonstrates mastery.

Challenges may include AI feedback, badges, or certificates.

---

## Learning Outcome

A measurable ability the learner gains after completing a Guide or Mission.

Always written as:

"After completing this Guide, you will be able to..."

---

## Interactive Guide

A Guide that combines explanation, examples, hands-on exercises, and reflection.

Cyberussell Guides are interactive by default.

---

## Reflection

A short activity where learners evaluate what they observed, learned, or would improve.

Reflection reinforces understanding and judgment.

---

## Workflow

A repeatable sequence of steps that combines AI tools and human decision-making to accomplish a task.

---

## AI Team

The collection of AI assistants introduced by Cyberussell.

Currently:
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

Future AI models should fit naturally into this concept.

---

## Cyberussell Tool

A utility that helps learners apply concepts.

Examples:
- Prompt Playground
- Resume Builder
- Website Planner
- Scam Checker

Tools support learning but do not replace Guides.

---

## Earn Path

A structured route that teaches learners how to monetize a specific skill.

Examples:
- Freelancing
- Website Business
- Affiliate Marketing
- Digital Products

---

## AI-First

A design principle where every skill is taught by showing how AI can accelerate, improve, or simplify the work before introducing traditional methods.

---

## Completion

A learner is considered to have completed a Guide only after finishing the interactive exercise and reflection.

Reading alone does not count as completion.
